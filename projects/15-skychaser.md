[Skychaser](https://skychaser.mesoview.app) is a global database of sky and weather observations. Users submit photos with time and location details, then browse reports on a map or in a feed.

Community-suggested tags help researchers and enthusiasts annotate cloud formations, storms, auroras, and other sky phenomena.
