Community weather platform

Skychaser

A global, community-powered database for sharing, browsing, and mapping photos of clouds, storms, and other sky phenomena.

Skychaser is a global database of sky and weather observations. Users submit photos with time and location details, then browse reports on a map or in a feed.

Community-suggested tags give a way for researchers and enthusiasts to share and annotate cloud formations, storms, auroras, and other sky phenomena.
