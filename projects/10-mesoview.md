Mesoview ([skychaser.mesoview.app](https://skychaser.mesoview.app)) is a mobile-friendly interface for viewing Storm Prediction Center mesoanalysis products. It improves on the usability of the official website and has gained traction in the storm chasing community.

The interface supports stacked parameter panels (for example 500 mb heights alongside surface vorticity and 0-3 km MLCAPE), time navigation, and regional sectors.

A major update in April 2026 ([announcement](https://x.com/rvandersmith/status/2041603976441856365)) added:

- Dark mode
- All SPC mesoanalysis products
- WPC surface analysis
- Pivotal Weather model data
- Point soundings
- Shareable URLs for real-time and archived data
